## In a law practice, confidentiality is a professional duty

A privacy program protects personal data against a regulator's rules. A law firm's duty is older
and wider: New Jersey RPC 1.6 covers all information relating to the representation, whatever its
source and whether or not anyone would call it private, and it survives the end of the engagement
and the death of the client. Privilege is narrower — it protects communications made for legal
advice from compelled disclosure — and it is lost by ordinary carelessness: a client's relative in
the meeting, advice forwarded to a beneficiary, a file shared with a lender.

The design consequences for a practice:

- **The client file is the data map.** Every asset list, deed, account number, health directive
  and family history the firm holds is confidential by rule. Treat the whole matter as the
  highest class rather than classifying field by field.
- **Vendors and AI tools are disclosures.** Before client information reaches a cloud service, an
  AI tool or a contractor, the firm has decided it may, on the basis of the vendor's retention,
  security and data-use terms. The New Jersey Supreme Court's 2026 model AI policy asks a firm to
  pick one confidentiality rule and write it down — no AI, no client information in public tools,
  de-identified only, or approved vendor tools only — and to check each tool's sharing and
  training settings before use.
- **Nothing identifying goes into a code repository, a shared template or a log.** Structure,
  templates, schemas and a log that records acts without names are what a firm's systems may
  carry; a filled client manifest, a generated instrument or a real Matter's facts are client data
  and live only where the client file lives. Test with fictional matters, never with a real one.
- **Retention is stated, not assumed.** The engagement letter says what the client receives at the
  end, what the firm keeps and for how long. Trust account records are kept seven years under
  R. 1:21-6; set the file-retention period in writing and run the deletion when it arrives.
- **A breach is also an ethics event.** Beyond any notification statute, the attorney tells an
  affected client what happened to their information, because the duty to communicate does not
  wait for a regulator's clock.
